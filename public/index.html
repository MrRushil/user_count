        Common questions

        Does Vasora AI give medical advice?

        No. Vasora AI gives nutrition guidance built around your conditions, using published dietary guidelines. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything, it never gives insulin or medication advice, and it doesn't replace your care team. This preview shows sample results only.

        What happens to my photo?

        In this preview it never leaves your phone. We'll explain how photos are handled before the real AI launches.

        What are the recipes?

        After you take the survey and snap your fridge, Vasora AI builds recipes from what you already have, made for your conditions and the foods you like. That is why the survey asks about both.

        Can Vasora AI help with weight or fat loss?

        Yes. You can set a goal to lose weight or lose body fat, and the guidance factors in your conditions. It's for adults, and anyone with an eating disorder history should talk to their doctor first.

        Why ask for height, weight and race?

        These help tailor guidance to your body and risk factors. Race is optional.
